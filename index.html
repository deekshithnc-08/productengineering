myPAL Clinic CRM

From patient outreach to clinic visits

Challenge. Clinic teams need to coordinate outreach, appointment booking and patient visits across marketing, reception and clinical staff.

Workflow. Marketing teams can prepare appointment campaigns with WhatsApp templates and booking links, then review campaign reports. At the clinic, staff can register patients with a unique health ID, manage bookings and check-ins, and track follow-ups. Role-based access and separate clinic workspaces support each team’s work.

Scope. The supplied UAT guides document these workflows. They do not report measured outcomes.
